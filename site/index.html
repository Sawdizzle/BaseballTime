Divisions come from each org's own cutoff rule, so a birthdate can land in two different ages depending on who runs the tournament. Tap a division to filter the board to it.

Weather shows for weekend events inside the next 16 days, which is as far as any forecast reaches. A Turf tag means the organizer advertises turf, which usually plays through rain when grass fields get called off — no organizer publishes the surface as data, so no tag means we don't know, not that it's grass. Counts come straight from each organizer and are re-checked twice a day. PAC events show total registrations across all ages (marked ≈) because PAC doesn't publish per-division team lists. Distances are straight-line from your chosen location, not driving miles.

Built and run by one dad in Sanger. If it saves you a Saturday of searching, a coffee keeps the scraper running. Questions, corrections, or a tournament we're missing? info@youthbaseballtime.com.
